| Decree date | 10/8/1442H |
| Related authority | Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture (MEWA) |
| Legal source | Council of Ministers' Bureau of Experts |

### Scope of the Law

The law regulates a number of areas, including:

- The Agricultural Register.
- Agricultural licenses and activities.
- Agricultural products.
- Control and inspection.
- Pesticides and veterinary preparations.
- Agricultural and veterinary waste.
- Plant resources.
- Animal resources.
- Living aquatic resources.
- Organic agriculture.
- Quarantine and protection from pests and diseases.
- Violations and penalties.

### Selected Excerpts

> **Article One:** The agricultural sector is the sector concerned with plant resources, animal resources, and living aquatic resources.

> **Article One:** The Ministry is the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture, and the Minister is the Minister of Environment, Water and Agriculture.

# 3. Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

## 3.1 Personal Data Protection Law

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Law name | Personal Data Protection Law |
| Abbreviation | PDPL |
| Status | **Active** |
| Original Decree | M/19 |
| Decree date | 9/2/1443H |
| Amendment Decree | M/148 |
| Amendment date | 5/9/1444H |
| Related authority | Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) |

### Scope of Application – Article 2

> The Law applies to any processing of personal data related to individuals that takes place in the Kingdom by any means, including the processing of data related to individuals residing in the Kingdom by any entity outside the Kingdom.

### Data Subject Rights – Article 4

Rights established by the Law include:

- The right to be informed of the reason and purpose of data collection.
- The right to access personal data.
- The right to obtain it in a clear and readable format.
- The right to request correction, completion, or update.
- The right to request destruction of the data when no longer needed, subject to statutory exceptions.

### Data Collection – Article 10

As a rule, the controller must collect personal data directly from its owner and process it for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to exceptions specified by the Law.

### Privacy Policy – Article 12

> The controller must adopt a personal data privacy policy and make it available to data subjects before collecting their data.

The policy clarifies, per the Law's requirements, the purpose of collection, the types of data, and how it is collected, stored, processed, and destroyed, along with the data subject's rights and how to exercise them.

### Disclosure – Article 15

Personal data may not be disclosed except within the cases and controls specified by the Law.

### Cross-Border Data Transfer – Article 29

The Law regulates the transfer of personal data or its disclosure to an entity outside the Kingdom, setting conditions and controls related to protection, vital interests, national security, and the minimum amount of data transferred.

### Penalties – Article 35

In cases of disclosure or publication of sensitive data in violation of the Law, if done with intent to harm the data subject or to achieve personal benefit, the penalty may reach:

- Imprisonment for a period not exceeding two years.
- A fine not exceeding SAR 3,000,000.
- Or either of the two penalties.

# 3.2 PDPL Executive Regulation

The Executive Regulation clarifies how a number of PDPL requirements are implemented.

### Data Subject Rights

The Regulation specifies the procedures for exercising rights and the response time for requests.

Among the key rules is that the controlling entity must fulfill requests within a period not exceeding **30 days**, with the possibility of extension for an additional period in cases permitted by the Regulation, with notice to the data subject.

### Privacy Policy

The Regulation clarifies the information that must be provided to the data subject, including:

- The identity of the controller.
- Contact details.
- The data protection officer, if any.
- The purpose of processing.
- The legal basis.
- The retention period or criteria for determining it.
- The data subject's rights.
- How to exercise those rights.
- How to withdraw consent.

### Data Security

The controller must take appropriate organizational, administrative, and technical measures to protect personal data.

### Records of Processing Activities

The Regulation specifies the requirements for maintaining a record of personal data processing activities, including the statutory retention period for the record after the processing activity ends.

# 4. Applying PDPL to the Agricultural Sector

If an agricultural project uses an application, platform, or digital system that collects personal data, **both agricultural regulations and PDPL may apply simultaneously**.

## Examples of Data That May Be Personal Data

- Farmer's name.
- National ID number.
- Mobile phone number.
- Email address.
- Contact information.
- License or record data linked to a natural person.
- Banking data.
- Geographic location when linked to and identifiable of a person.
- Photos of individuals.
- Employee data.
- Customer and supplier data of natural persons.

## Suggested Practical Requirements

When designing a digital agricultural system, the following should be examined:

1. Determine the purpose of collecting each type of data.
2. Collect the minimum necessary data.
3. Provide a clear privacy policy.
4. Determine the data retention period.
5. Establish destruction procedures once the data is no longer needed.
6. Control access permissions.
7. Document processing activities where requirements apply.
8. Assess the need for a data protection officer.
9. Regulate the relationship with technical service providers that process the data.
10. Review any cross-border data transfer in accordance with the Law and the transfer regulation.
